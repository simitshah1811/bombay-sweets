# Bombay Sweets — Hero Sequence Asset Shot List

Everything below is scoped to the new cinematic hero (Hero.tsx replacement). Nothing else on the site changes. Consistent lighting/backdrop across all stills matters a lot — treat it like one photoshoot, not several.

**House backdrop:** warm ivory/cream (`#FBF3E6`) or muted peach (`#F3E2C9`), seamless, no visible horizon line, soft diffused lighting (no harsh direct shadows, gentle rim light to separate the subject from the background).

---

## 1. HERO SWEET — 2 stills (no video needed)

Pick one visually striking sweet — a diamond-cut **Kaju Katli** or a glossy **Gulab Jamun** both work well; something with texture that reads at a distance. The hero sequence is a scroll-driven push-in → macro reveal → release (camera does the work, not the object), so it needs exactly two photos of this one sweet:

1. **Full shot** — "A single [sweet], centered, on a plain warm ivory background (#FBF3E6), soft diffused studio lighting, gentle rim light to define edges, sharp focus, photorealistic product photography, isolated subject."
2. **Macro/texture shot of the same sweet** — extreme close-up of its surface (e.g. pistachio crumb topping, syrup sheen, or a cut cross-section showing layers) — see the macro prompts below, pick whichever matches this sweet.

*(These get cross-faded and scaled by GSAP as you scroll — the "camera pushes into the texture" moment.)*

---

## 2. MACRO TEXTURE CLOSE-UPS — 3–5 stills

For the "camera pushes into the texture" moment.

1. Pistachio crumb topping, extreme close-up, shallow depth of field
2. Saffron strands — a few threads on a dark or cream surface
3. Glossy syrup/glaze sheen (gulab jamun surface, or a barfi's cut edge)
4. Cross-section cut of a barfi/katli showing internal layers
5. *(optional)* Cardamom pods, one cracked open

---

## 3. COLLECTION SWEETS — 5–6 individual photos

Same backdrop/lighting as the hero video for visual consistency. Straight-on or gentle 3/4 angle. **Transparent/isolated background if possible** (easier to layer with parallax depth).

1. Gulab Jamun
2. Rasgulla
3. Besan Barfi
4. Kaju Katli *(skip if used as the hero video subject)*
5. Milk Cake
6. Boondi Ladoo

---

## 4. INGREDIENT SHOTS — 6–8 stills

Small, simple compositions — a pile or a few pieces, not a full dish. Same clean backdrop.

1. Shelled pistachios (small pile)
2. Almonds (whole + a couple sliced)
3. Saffron strands (small pinch)
4. Cardamom pods (whole + a couple cracked)
5. Dried rose petals
6. Milk (a pour, or a small bowl)
7. Sugar or khoya (fresh milk solids)

---

## 5. HANDCRAFT SECTION — 2–4 photos, or a short clip

Close-up of hands shaping, rolling, or cutting a sweet — e.g. rolling a ladoo, cutting barfi into diamonds, dusting pistachio on top. Warm kitchen lighting, shallow depth of field. This reads as evocative craft imagery, not a documentary claim about specific staff — so a generic/styled shoot is fine, doesn't need to be *your* actual kitchen if that's easier to arrange.
